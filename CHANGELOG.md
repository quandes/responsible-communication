# Changelog

## 0.3.0 – 2026-10-04

- One skill only: `responsible-communication` (English). The German skill variant `responsible-communication-de` has been removed to avoid maintaining two versions. If you installed it, replace it with `skills/responsible-communication`; reports can still be requested in German.
- German README remains (`README.de.md`).

## 0.2.0 – 2026-10-04

- First public release under the MIT license.
- Two self-contained variants: `responsible-communication` (English, canonical) and `responsible-communication-de` (German translation).
- Modes `reflect` (default) and `review`; `guardrail` is experimental and not active.
- Reports are written in the language of the request; quotes stay in the draft's language.
- Test cases describe expected behavior; independent multi-model runs are pending.

## 0.1.0 – 2026-10-04

- Internal prototype (German), not published.
