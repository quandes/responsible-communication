# Review report

**Mode:** reflect / review

**Version and scope:** document version, parts reviewed, sources read, missing context.

**Overall observation:** short observation. No moral score, no claim of approval.

## Finding RC-01

**Passage:** short exact quote from the reviewed text, with paragraph number if useful.

**Criterion / priority / confidence:** each with a traceable reason.

**Observation and possible effect:** what is written, what may follow from it? No assumed intent.

**Evidence status:** supported / open / contradicted / not applicable, with an accessible source.

**Reflection question:** one concrete question that improves a decision.

**Alternative:** only in review mode; mark it as a suggestion.

## Closing

Open points, unchecked statements and the human decision that is needed.
Original unchanged. Nothing published. No guarantee of correctness.
