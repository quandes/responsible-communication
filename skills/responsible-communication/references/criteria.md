# Criteria and boundaries

| ID | Review question | Do not flag automatically |
|---|---|---|
| dignity | Are people denied equal worth or belonging as human beings? | Sharp criticism of decisions or behavior |
| generalization | Is a shared trait attributed to a heterogeneous group without basis? | Precisely delimited, supported group statistics |
| evidence | Does the available source support the factual claim? | Clearly marked opinion, experience or open hypothesis |
| fear_pressure | Is a threat exaggerated without support, or is pressure created instead of information? | Supported risk communication and comprehensible warnings |
| manipulation | Are readers steered by deception, guilt or false promises of belonging? | Recognizable advertising, reasoned persuasion and emotion |
| context | Does an omission, misleading quote or image caption change the statement? | Not every unmentioned perspective; no demand for completeness |
| privacy | Are private data, experiences or consents used without support? | Specific, purpose-bound approval by the people concerned |
| status | Is an offer, an effect or an approval claimed to exist? | Clearly named draft or pilot status |

Confidence describes how robust a finding is, not the moral worth of the author.
High priority: clear degradation, relevant fabricated facts, significant privacy risks.
Medium priority: context-dependent distortion with a plausible effect.
Low priority: limited ambiguity. Never report matters of taste as serious violations.
