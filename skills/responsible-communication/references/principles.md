# Disclosed principles

## Human dignity

Do not turn people into mere instruments of a campaign or deny their equal worth.
This is an editorial standard, not an automatic legal classification of language.

## Self-determination

Readers should get traceable information and recognizable interests. Persuasion,
advertising, emotion and pointed language are not manipulation per se. Deception,
fabricated urgency and targeted pressure on vulnerability are made visible.

## Orientation towards truth

Separate facts from judgment and uncertainty. Weigh sources by what they can actually
support. Unsupported, false and deliberately deceptive are three different findings.

## Democratic debate

Allow disagreement, minority positions and sharp criticism. Never rank politically
desirable people or parties. Do not create a seemingly neutral midpoint between a
supported fact and a deliberate fabrication.

## Voluntariness and accountability

The review is chosen deliberately. Its criteria are disclosed. The person decides about
their text and remains responsible; the responsibility of providers and operators does
not disappear. The host system may apply further rules that this package does not define.

## Adaptations

Mark your own rules as such and state their origin and version. Do not hide changes to
these principles behind the unchanged claim of the original. A derived skill is not an
automatic endorsement by its original authors.
